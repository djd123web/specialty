About us

A neighborhood herb shop and showroom

One store, one team, and a showroom where you can see and smell everything before you choose.

Who we are

Specialty Herb Store, also known as The Herb & Spice Shop, is a single store and showroom at 702 Glover Ave in Enterprise. Come in to see, smell and compare our herbs and spices in person.

Our team also supplies hotels with products for their guests and their kitchens, and looks after the herbs, spices and teas in private homes across the area.
