Protect yourself from hiring scams

Scammers sometimes pretend to be employers in order to collect personal information from job seekers, such as Social Security numbers. You should not need to share your Social Security number or other sensitive personal information to apply for a job.

If you are in doubt about a message, a call or a job offer, email careers@specialtyherbstore.com before you reply.
