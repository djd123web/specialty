Questions and answers

What do I get each week?

A visit from our team during your visit day. We take away what you have finished and set out a new seasonal selection of herbs, spices, teas and dried botanicals for the areas covered by your plan.

Who chooses the products?

Our team chooses the weekly selection, guided by the tastes, allergies and preferences you share with us at your home consultation. You can change them at any time.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Monthly plans can be paused or cancelled with 14 days notice before your next billing date. Annual plans can be cancelled before the renewal date.

Do I need to be home for the visit?

No. Tell us how to enter and about any pets or alarms, and we will let ourselves in at your chosen time window, or you can be there to let us in.

What if someone in my household has allergies?

Tell us at signup and at any time. We keep these notes on file and choose products with them in mind. Please keep reading product labels as usual.

How do I pay?

By credit card. Monthly plans are charged each month on the date you join. Annual plans are charged once a year and save 10%.
